`IMAGES:` to find the exact spot, then replace the comment with an
`<img>` or an `.image-grid` block (see **Into A Dream** for the pattern).

- **Novar** (`#novar`) — a Novar world map, and 2–3 wiki screenshots (nation page, timeline).
- **Flow Writer** (`#flow-writer`) — a screenshot of the VS Code extension and of the desktop editor.
- **Novar Wiki** (`#novar-wiki`) — 2–3 wiki screenshots — a nation page, an index page, the timeline.
- **War Doctor — Website** (`#war-doctor-website`) — a screenshot of the site's hero section.
- **City Map Generator** (`#city-map-generator`) — 2–3 generated city maps at different seeds. Good candidate for a thumbnail.
