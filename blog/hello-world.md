# Hello, World — Why I Made This

Every portfolio needs a first post. Mine is overdue.

I've built a lot of things — games, plays, songs, systems, words, worlds. For years I kept them scattered: a link here, a folder there, a SoundCloud nobody asked for. The idea of putting it all in one place felt presumptuous. *Who needs that?*

Turns out, I do.

## Why Now

The practical answer is boring: I needed somewhere to point people. The real answer is that I've spent too long apologizing for having too many interests. I am a designer **and** a developer **and** an engineer **and** a writer **and** a musician **and** a builder of fictional worlds. The "and" isn't a liability. It's the whole point.

This site is me refusing to pick one thing.

## What You'll Find Here

The **Work** section has everything I'm willing to show publicly — games, theatre, music, writing, technical projects, and worldbuilding. Most of it is real; some of it is in progress; all of it is honest.

The **Blog** is where I write about process. Not tutorials. Not "10 tips for designers." Just thinking out loud about what it's like to be this many things at once.

## What Comes Next

A lot, actually. There are projects I haven't finished. Worlds I haven't mapped. Songs I haven't recorded. This site will grow as those things do.

In the meantime: welcome. Have a look around. If something resonates, [get in touch](/#contact).
