I build worlds from nothing and make them feel inevitable.

I'm a designer, developer, engineer, writer, musician, and builder of fictional worlds. Not sequentially: simultaneously. I make video games and theatre. I write code and prose. I compose music and engineer systems. The throughline is making things that didn't exist before.

I believe the best work happens at intersections. The game designer who writes dialogue. The engineer who thinks in metaphors. The musician who maps fictional continents. That's the space I live in, and this site is proof of it.
